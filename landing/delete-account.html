Delete your account and data

Last updated: 18 August 2026

You can delete your TrackKcal account and everything in it at any time. There are two ways to do it. You do not need a reason and we will not try to talk you out of it.

In the app, immediately

This is the fastest way and it takes effect straight away.

  1. Open TrackKcal
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

By email, if you no longer have the app

Email TrackKcal@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and say you want your account deleted. Using the same email address is how we confirm the request is really yours. If you cannot email from that address, tell us and we will find another way to verify you.

We action deletion requests within 30 days and normally within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your food log, including any photos you logged
  • Your weight, water, and exercise entries
  • Your goals, targets, diet, and preferences
  • Your conversations with the AI Nutritionist
  • Your notification token, so we stop being able to message you

What does not get deleted, and why

  • Anonymised usage and crash records. These cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing personal left to delete.
  • Data already sent to an AI provider. When you logged food or talked to the coach, that request went to OpenAI or Moonshot AI. It is held under their own retention policy and we cannot reach into their systems to recall it.
  • Records we are required to keep. If you bought a subscription, transaction records may be retained where the law requires it. These do not include your health data.
  • Shared journal entries. If you also use TrackLifts, weight and water entries recorded by that app belong to that app and are deleted through it. Deleting TrackKcal removes the entries TrackKcal wrote.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Subscriptions before you delete, or you will keep being billed.

Just want a copy instead?

Email TrackKcal@gmail.com and ask for an export. We will send you everything we hold on you.
