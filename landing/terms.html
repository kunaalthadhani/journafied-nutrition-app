What TrackKcal is

TrackKcal is a mobile application that helps you log what you eat, estimate calories and nutrients using AI, and track your weight and health goals over time. It is a tool. It is not a doctor, a dietitian, or a licensed medical professional, and it is not a medical device.

Health and safety

Read this part properly.

  • Nothing in TrackKcal is medical advice. The AI Nutritionist, the weekly review, Smart Suggest, and every calorie or macro target are general information generated from the data you logged.
  • Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet, particularly if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are recovering from illness, or take medication affected by food.
  • If you have or have had an eating disorder, calorie tracking can be harmful. This app counts everything you eat and shows you the number. That is the opposite of what many people in recovery need. Please speak to a professional before using it, and stop using it if it is making things worse.
  • The calorie targets the app calculates are estimates from a standard formula. They are not prescriptions. Do not eat below a target a professional has set for you because an app suggested a lower one.

AI estimates are estimates

TrackKcal uses AI to estimate calorie and nutrient content from the description or photo you provide. These figures are not measured. They are best guesses, often very good ones, sometimes wrong. Where the app finds a manufacturer's published label it uses those figures instead, and those are only as accurate as the label. Do not rely on any of it as the sole basis for a medical, clinical, or dietary decision.

The AI can also be confidently wrong. If a number looks wrong to you, it may well be. Correct it. You can edit any entry.
